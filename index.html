        8FAQ / Trade Help

        Quick reference only. While in store, confirm trade values with designated AT&T trade tools and verify promotion eligibility in OPUS/CCKM before quoting.

        1. How long until promo credits hit the account?

        Promo credits usually appear within 1–3 billing cycles. If credits do not show right away, the customer should keep paying the bill normally. Credits may catch up later if the promotion and trade were completed correctly.

        2. How long does the customer have to trade in the phone?

        Customers usually have 30 days to complete the trade-in. Best practice is to complete it same day, within 14 days, or at least within the same billing month whenever possible.

        3. What is the difference between trade value and promotion value?

        The trade value is what the trade system says the phone is worth. That value determines which promotion tier the customer qualifies for. A phone can have a lower actual trade value but still qualify for a larger bill-credit promotion.

        4. Do we quote the promo before checking trade value?

        No. While in store, confirm the device value using designated AT&T trade tools first. This calculator and the tradein.att.com reference link are not the final in-store valuation source. Value can change based on condition, activation lock, exact model, storage, and current trade rules.

        5. Who sends the trade-in phones?

        We send the phones through the approved trade-in process when completing the trade. Double-check the trade device, customer information, IMEI/model, and submission details before sending.

        6. Why does the tool say to verify in OPUS/CCKM?

        This tool is a sales guide and estimate. Promotions, eligible devices, plan requirements, and trade rules can change. OPUS, CCKM, and the approved trade flow are the final checks before quoting or completing the sale.

        7. How does the customer’s plan affect eligible promotions?

        The customer’s plan is one of the main indicators of which promotions they may be eligible for. Premium, Starter, Value, 55+, FirstNet, and CRU plan groups can qualify differently, so confirm the plan group before quoting.

        8. Can damaged or destroyed phones still qualify?

        Sometimes, yes. Damaged and sometimes even destroyed phones can still qualify depending on the current promotion and the value returned by the approved trade flow. Do not assume based on appearance alone — check the trade system before quoting.

        9. How can I tell the difference between Next Up Anytime and legacy Next Up on a customer's bill?

        Check the feature name and monthly feature charge on the bill or in OPUS. Next Up Anytime usually shows as $10/mo. Legacy Next Up may show as $6/mo. In the main tool, choose the matching feature so the promo path calculates correctly. Next Up Anytime uses the 33% paid path: if it is 33%+ paid, use the normal promo path; if it is under 33%, AT&T can still pay off the remaining installments for the upgrade path, but no new phone promo applies. Legacy Next Up uses the 50% paid path. Verify the feature, payoff percentage, device condition, and offer eligibility in OPUS/CCKM.

        10. Do legacy Next Up lines get promos?

        Yes, if the offer allows it. Legacy Next Up uses a 50% paid check. If the customer is under 50%, they generally need to pay the installment balance up to 50% first, then they can use the legacy Next Up upgrade path. Next Up Anytime is different: at 33%+ paid, use the normal promo path; under 33%, AT&T payoff path only and no new phone promo applies. Always verify in OPUS/CCKM before quoting.

        11. What should reps double-check before submitting the order?

        Confirm the correct plan group, promo, device SKU/storage, trade value, trade deadline, Next Up status, customer expectations, and OPUS/CCKM eligibility before quoting or completing the sale.

        📘 Management How-To Guide
        The #1 rule: If an offer ends, use Active → Off or its end date whenever possible. Avoid deleting real offers. Keeping old records gives us history and makes mistakes easier to undo.

        📱 Trade-In Phones

        Use this when a trade-in phone's value changes or a missing trade phone needs to be added. Search the phone, select it, change the value, stage it, then tap Apply Changes. For a new phone, tap + Add and enter Brand, Model, Storage and Trade Value.

        📱 Devices: Sold vs Trade-In

        Phones We Sell is now the main device record. Sellable decides whether the phone appears under “Phone Getting.” Trade Eligible decides whether that same SKU can be selected as a trade-in, and Trade Value stores its current reference value. When a phone stops being sold, turn Sellable off instead of deleting it so it can remain a trade-in later.

        🏷️ Tiered Offers

        Tiered Prices controls special monthly offers. New-line and upgrade pricing are separate records. Eligible Segments accepts comma-separated values like consumer or consumer,cru,firstnet. If an offer ends, deactivate/end the applicable price rather than deleting the phone.

        💰 Promotions

        Use Promotions for the overall offer and Trade Promo Tiers for its dollar thresholds. Example: a tier can say a $290+ trade receives $1,100. Before changing several related promo records, verify the official offer first.

        🧾 Plans

        Use Plans to add, rename or deactivate a wireless plan. Plan Groups determine which promotion family a plan belongs to. If you're only changing the displayed plan name, don't change its group unless promo eligibility also changed.

        📰 What's New

        Use What's New / Content for a short management-facing description of meaningful changes. Customers/reps will see What's New once per day on each browser after the app is configured to display the current Supabase content.

        ✅ After Applying

        Review the pending changes, tap Apply Changes, and Admin refreshes the live data without reloading the page. Run one quote that uses the thing you changed.
